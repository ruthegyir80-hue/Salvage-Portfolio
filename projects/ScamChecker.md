# ScamChecker — Instant Scam Risk Analyzer

Live demo: https://agent-6a67ddd85e5263008503--fancy-kringle-d660e4.netlify.app/

## Short description
A public, no-signup web tool that analyzes pasted messages, links, or emails and returns an instant risk rating and recommended next steps to help users identify potential scams and phishing attempts.

## What it does
- Accepts pasted text, suspect URLs, or email content and analyzes for common scam and phishing indicators.
- Returns an immediate risk rating (e.g., Low / Medium / High) and concise next-step advice.
- Designed for quick, on-the-spot checks — no account required.
